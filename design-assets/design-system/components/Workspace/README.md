# Workspace

A side chat on the left and the step's work on the right, for the project conversation at wide width. You provide the messages, the reply box and the work.

- The project conversation at wide width (1024 px and up), adopted for every desktop conversation screen on November 13. Below 1024 px the one-column conversation in the reading container is used.
- **The chat talks, the right shows the work** (chosen November 12). The chat holds the brief, Embel's messages and everything about status: the progress bar, and an error with its one button. The work column holds only what the person judges or chooses. While something is being made the work column shows skeletons in the final shape; after a failure it shows a quiet empty state with no button.
- **Finished steps stay in the chat** (chosen November 12), as one-line folded entries with one answer each and their Edit button inside.
- **Chat panel** (`e-workspace__chat`): `workspace-chat-width` wide (400 px by default), paper with a `color-border` hairline and `radius-panel`, no shadow, floating 16 px from the window's edges so it reads as above the canvas. A header (`e-workspace__head`) carries the name "Chat" and the Hide chat icon button (`panel-left-close`, with a tooltip). It holds the message log (`e-workspace__log`, the only part that scrolls, with the messages in one `e-workspace__thread` so the newest stays in view) and the reply box (`e-workspace__reply`, pinned to the bottom, not floating).
- **Work area** (`e-workspace__main`): everything the person judges or chooses, on the canvas. It runs from the handle to the window's right edge and scrolls on its own (its scrollbar at the window's edge); its content is centred in it and never wider than the page width.
- **The layout fills the window** (changed November 13): there is no maximum width, so the panel is always 16 px (`nav-offset`) from the window's left, top and bottom edges, at any window size. The chat and the work area scroll separately, as in other apps with a side chat; the page itself does not scroll. In a window under 600 px tall the whole page scrolls instead, so fixed chrome stays the bar alone.
- **Project bar** (changed November 13): its wrapper takes `e-nav-wrap--workspace`, which centres the floating bar over the work area instead of the whole window. Between 1024 and 1279 px the four steps collapse into the steps menu button, because the work column is too narrow for all four names.
- Inside the panel a message has no avatar column and the person's own message sits on `color-bg-sunken`.
- Below 1024 px there is no side chat: the layout is one column, chat first.
- **Resize** (added November 13): a handle (`e-workspace__resize`) in the gap between the chat and the work. Drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to set the chat between 320 and 560 px; the app writes the width to `--workspace-chat-width`, so the work column and the bar follow. At rest it is a 4 × 40 px grip in `color-border-control` (3.26:1 on the canvas); on hover, focus and while dragging (`data-state="dragging"`) it turns ink. It is a `role="separator"` with `aria-orientation="vertical"`, `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`, and a 44 px target.
- **Hide and show** (added November 13): Hide chat removes the panel and the handle (`e-workspace--chat-hidden`); the work area then takes the whole window, its content centred at page width, and the bar centres over it (its wrapper drops `e-nav-wrap--workspace`). A floating pill with "Show chat" (`e-workspace__show`, `panel-left-open`) takes the chat's place, level with the bar. The width and the hidden state are remembered for the person.
- While the chat is hidden, Embel's messages still arrive in it; the Show chat pill carries a counter when one is unread.
- **Stop is in the reply box** (changed November 13): while Embel is working, the reply box's Send button becomes a Stop button in the same place (`e-icon-button--primary`, `square` icon, a label and tooltip naming the work, such as "Stop drawing"). The progress bar in the chat carries no button. When the work ends, Send comes back. The same holds in the one-column conversation below 1024 px (owner decision, November 13): Stop replaces Send in the docked reply box, never in the progress row.
- **Results while the chat is resized** (owner decision, November 13, option 2 of 3): on steps whose results must all be in view (`e-workspace__main--rows-1`, `--rows-2`), the results keep the size the window's height allows while there is room, and shrink as the chat is widened, so they stay three across and in view. The chat's range stays 320 to 560 px at every window width. At 1440 × 900 a logo concept is 304 px with the chat at 400 px and 251 px at 560 px; at 1920 × 1080 it stays 336 px.
- **Results fit the window** (added November 13): a step whose results are judged side by side gives its work area `e-workspace__main--rows-2` (six logo concepts, two rows of three) or `e-workspace__main--rows-1` (three direction boards, one row). At wide width the results are three across and the column (heading and results) narrows so all of them are in view without scrolling, centred under the project bar. A result's size is capped by the window's height (`--result-size`).
- **Send moves to the next step** (owner decision, November 13; Logo and Creative direction steps, then applied to the Name step, Inspiration and the finished brand): there is no Continue button, in the work area or in the chat. Once a concept or a direction is chosen, the reply box holds it as a static Chip ("Logo: Concept 1 · Version 2") and Send is available: Send with nothing typed continues to the next step (its label and tooltip say "Continue to Creative direction"); with text typed, the text is a change to the chosen concept. Before a concept is chosen, Send stays unavailable. Without a Continue row the six concepts grow to fill the work area's width (`--result-size` is the window height less three `space-24`, halved).
- **A chosen result keeps its size** (added November 13): it stays in its place in the grid, marked with the selected border only (result tiles carry no check mark), and its button reads Chosen (pressed). Actions about the chosen result (its version menu, Download PNG) sit in the step's heading row.
- **Work-area overlay** (added November 13): a dialog about the work, such as a name's details, opens in `e-workspace__overlay`, which dims and covers only the work area (from the handle to the window's right edge). The chat stays in view and usable, so the dialog is not modal (`aria-modal` is false); Close or Escape returns to the work. Below 1024 px it covers the whole window.
- **Where the name list, the overlay and the reply box are described**: Name list, Dialog (name details) and Conversation (choices in the reply box).
