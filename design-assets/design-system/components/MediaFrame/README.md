# Media frame

A box with a fixed aspect ratio that holds a generated or uploaded image. You provide the ratio, the image and its alt text.

- Ratios: 1:1 (logos), 4:5 (direction boards in a grid), 16:9 (banners, large views), 8:11 (the brand board). Added November 12: 4:3 (the example brands on Home), 3:1 (the wide banner file) and 4:1 (the cover banner file).
- States: **loading** (a skeleton at the final size), **loaded** (the image fades in over `duration-base`), **failed** (an icon and one sentence inside; the retry Button sits beside the frame, never inside another button), **private or expired** (one wording: "This preview is private.").
- Logos use contain on paper so their own background is kept; boards and photos use cover.
- Every generated image sits in a Media frame. A generated brand's colours appear only inside frames like this, never as interface chrome.
- Controls drawn on the image sit on a paper chip (Icon button, on-artwork).
- **Drawing** (added November 13, used for logo concepts; fuller effect approved by the owner the same day): `e-media--drawing` instead of the plain skeleton. A dotted frame draws itself from the top centre outward, with four small handles on its corners (`e-media__handles`) and two mono labels on its top line: what the tile is (`e-media__label`, "Concept 4", top left) and what is happening (`e-media__status`, "Drawing", top right). A grey halftone screen sweeps slowly across and back for as long as the work runs. The frame keeps `role="img"` and a label such as "Concept 4 is being drawn".
- **Reveal** (owner decision, November 13): when the picture arrives, the frame takes `e-media--revealing` and a script draws the picture on `e-media__canvas`, laid exactly over it, from the picture's own pixels, in about 3 seconds: a contour **sketch** (1 s), a grey **screen** of dots sized by the picture's light and dark (1 s), the same dots in the picture's **colours** (0.6 s), then the picture **resolves** from a 6 px blur while the frame, handles, labels and dots fade (0.7 s). The status label names each stage ("Sketch", "Screen 423 dots", "Colour", "Resolving") and the tile's Enlarge button waits until the reveal ends. This is the "image" variant of transitions.dev's Generative effect; in the app, use that skill's own script (its licence allows use in the product). The canvas shows an approximation written for the design. With reduced motion, the dots are still and the picture fades in with no reveal. The simpler `e-media--reveal` (blur to sharp only) stays for pictures that load without the effect.
