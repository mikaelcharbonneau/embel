# Section bar

The anchors of a long page and its one action, in one bar. You provide the sections and the action.

- Used on Assets: "Brand images", "Brand guidelines", "Design system" on the left, "Download brand kit" on the right. One bar per page.
- The anchors are Tabs that move to a section of the same page; the current one is underlined. The action is one primary Button.
- Paper, `radius-card`, `shadow-raised` (it floats), 56 px tall.
- At wide width in a window at least 600 px tall it stays in view under the navigation. Anywhere smaller it sits in the flow at the top of the page, so fixed chrome never exceeds a quarter of a 450 px window.
- At compact width the anchors scroll sideways inside the bar and the action takes the full width under them.
- States are those of Tabs and Button; the bar has none of its own.
