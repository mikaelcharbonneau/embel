Logo

Give the logo clear space and keep it away from other text and images. On light backgrounds, use the copy with a transparent background (logo/logo-transparent.png). On dark backgrounds, use the one-color reversed version (logo/logo-reversed.png). Don't stretch the logo or change its colors otherwise.

Graphics

Supporting graphics for direction 1: Precision at altitude: deep navy and signal amber, a fine grid, flight-path lines and data points; example uses on a dashboard screen, a business card and a conference booth banner.

Composition

Composition for direction 1: generous margins, one focal point.

Using your files

PNG files are ready to upload. SVG social templates keep text, colors and shapes editable; their embedded logo is the original raster image. Any headline or message in them comes from your saved slogan and positioning; edit it for each post. The original logo file keeps its saved artwork and background. The transparent and reversed logos are raster copies made from it. The selected direction image is included separately as a visual reference.

Social crops vary by platform and device. Check placement in the destination platform before publishing.
