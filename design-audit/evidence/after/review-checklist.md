# Desktop redesign: final review

November 14, 2026. The approved redesign is built for desktop (1024 px and wider) and live on www.embel.xyz. This file records the last check: the acceptance criteria in section 8 of `../../Design_AUDIT.md`, measured, and the section 9 review checklist, one line per finding. Evidence paths are relative to this folder (`design-audit/evidence/after/`); the "before" evidence keeps the same names one level up.

Phone-only measures are out of scope and were not checked: Lighthouse mobile, 390 px and 320 px layouts, LCP and frame rate under phone conditions, touch-device hearts (D-061), the phone recording (D-083).

## What was measured (section 8, desktop)

| Measure | Target | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lighthouse accessibility and best practices, desktop | 100 and 100 on every route | 100 / 100 on 39 of 42 local states and on production Home. The 404 page can't be scored (Lighthouse refuses a 404 response). | axe, WCAG A and AA | No violation | None on 42 states at 1440 px. The only best-practice flag is "region" on signed-out Projects (the skip link, a timing artefact). | `a11y/<state>__1440.json` |
| Contrast, reflow at 200% zoom | 4.5:1 text, 3:1 parts; no sideways scroll | 0 text-contrast failures; no sideways scroll or clipping at 200% zoom | `a11y/summary.json`, `a11y/reflow/` |
| Keyboard and focus | Focus never on the page body after an action; dialogs and menus return focus | All 16 scenarios run. Every dialog and menu returns focus to its opener; a wrong password focuses the password field; reopening a finished step focuses its panel. The only "body" stop is Tab past the end of the page from the non-modal larger view. | `a11y/keyboard.json`, `annotated/A-kbd-*.png` |
| Layout shift (CLS), production | 0.1 or less | Home 0.00; brand conversations 0.012 to 0.025; Assets 0.010 (signed in, 1440 px) | `traces/home__signed-out__desktop__load.json.gz`; measured in the browser |

| Console | No errors | None on any production page checked, signed out (8 routes) and signed in (Home, Projects, a brand, Assets, Library, Plans, Settings) | post-release check |
| Reduced motion | Movement removed, waiting feedback stays | Press and entrance movement off; the board reveal becomes a 120 ms fade; waiting pulses keep running | `motion/probe.json` (animationsReduced) |

One risk to note: on the local development server, a brand at the Logo or Creative direction step briefly shows the Inspiration gallery while its data loads, then swaps to the step (CLS 0.15 to 0.25 there). Production loads fast enough that it does not register (0.02), but a slow connection could show it.
