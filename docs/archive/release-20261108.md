## Database

One guarded owner transaction applied, in order:

1. `migrations/20261021050000_inspiration_logo_studies.sql`: never installed in
   production before. It adds `public.get_inspiration_reference_ids()`, without
   which every new brand would be browse-only, plus 15 retired catalog rows.
2. `migrations/20261106150000_guided_journey_stages.sql` (#193).
3. `candidates/shared_guided_journey_stages.sql` (#193).
4. `candidates/shared_naming_brainstorm_continuation.sql` (#196).
5. `migrations/20261108120000_inspiration_brands.sql` (#197, as fixed in #198):
   56 brand rows. Old-design likes were cleared on live projects only: 3 likes
   on 1 project. The 9 likes on 4 deleted projects were left untouched.
6. Two `fluid_private.shared_worker_policies` rows for the Strategy slot
   (`strategy.positioning`, `strategy.slogan`), copied from `strategy.audience`.
7. Migration history rows for the three migrations.

Before the bodies, the transaction locked the slot registry (exclusive) and the
protected job, accounting and Naming tables (share), checked the drain, and
recorded table hashes. After the bodies, it refused to commit unless all 293
authority functions matched the new manifest, the Strategy slot had exactly six
policies (Audience, Positioning and Slogan identical), and the protected hashes
were unchanged.
